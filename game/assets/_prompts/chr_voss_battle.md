Row 2 — battle poses, all facing LEFT: 1) battle idle with gauntlet ready, 2) idle venting steam, 3) attacking — piston punch forward to the left with a burst of steam, 4) hurt — staggered but barely.
Pixel scale: draw each figure as a small native sprite about 44 pixels wide and 60 pixels tall, then upscale with nearest-neighbour so each art pixel becomes a large perfectly square block.
