# npc_villagers2 — Pip, Guarda Olavo, Velha Ilse, Sr. Anselmo (+ soldado imperial no mapa)

```text
Asset type: NPC reference sheet for townsfolk of a steampunk frontier workshop town, to be converted into in-game sprites.
Composition: a strict grid of 5 rows x 4 columns, same scale, same baseline per row, lots of empty background between figures.
Row 1 — PIP: a lively 9-year-old boy, messy blond hair, oversized brown newsboy cap, red scarf, patched blue shorts and suspenders, soot on his cheek. Smaller than adults.
Row 2 — GUARD OLAVO: a chubby good-natured town guard, big brown moustache, dented brass helmet, faded green uniform coat, holding a wooden halberd.
Row 3 — OLD ILSE: a tiny hunched old woman, white hair in a bun, round spectacles, lavender shawl, grey dress, walking cane.
Row 4 — MR. ANSELMO: a thin elderly clockmaker, bald with white side hair, magnifying loupe over one eye, dark green waistcoat with many pocket watches on chains, cream shirt.
Row 5 — IMPERIAL SOLDIER: imperial navy-blue uniform coat with brass buttons, brass-rimmed helmet with dark goggles covering the eyes, holding a long steam rifle.
Columns for every row: 1) facing down/front, 2) facing left/side, 3) facing up/back, 4) facing down/front in a characteristic gesture (Pip jumping, Olavo saluting, Ilse leaning on cane, Anselmo checking a pocket watch, soldier aiming).
Pixel scale: draw each adult as a small native sprite about 36 pixels wide and 52 pixels tall (Pip and Ilse smaller), then upscale with nearest-neighbour so each art pixel becomes a large perfectly square block.
```
